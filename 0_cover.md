\#\# Purpose of the Assignment

This assignment investigates how the mean and median describe the typical price of an item and how the choice of average can affect the interpretation of data.

It also examines first-to-last percentage change to describe how prices changed over the observation period.

The assignment uses an Interpretation Auditor to review AI-generated claims, checks those claims against the original data, and explores why the mean is not always the best measure of a typical value.

\#\# Dataset Description

The dataset contains 96 observations covering four items across two cities over 12 weekly dates.

The recorded prices are in illustrative PKR per kg. The dataset is labelled as illustrative teaching data and does not represent actual Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) observations.

\#\# Main Methods

\- Arithmetic mean  
\- Median  
\- First-to-last percentage change  
\- Comparison of mean and median  
\- Verification of AI-generated interpretations  
\- Classification of observations, explanations and causal claims
